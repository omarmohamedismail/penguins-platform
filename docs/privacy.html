Your workspace data

Penguins is currently a development platform. Your login is handled by Supabase Auth. Workspace records and up to ten recent snapshots are stored in the connected Supabase database and isolated by your account ID.

Unsaved drafts are kept in this browser on this device. Sign out before sharing a device. Exported JSON and CSV backups contain your workspace records; keep them private.

Do not upload sensitive client health or financial records until the platform owner has completed its legal, privacy, and operational launch review. No payment processing, delivered messaging, electronic signatures, or real client portal access is included in this edition.

You can export your data from the workspace toolbar. Contact the platform owner for account or data deletion; self-service account deletion is not yet available.
